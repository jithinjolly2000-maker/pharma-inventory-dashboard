Reorder recommendations rule + judgement

Predicts demand over each supplier's lead time + safety stock. Rule flags "reorder now"; a human confirms the quantity.

Slow-moving stock days-of-cover + trend

Flags SKUs with days-of-cover above threshold, with a movement trend so it's not just a flat cutoff.

Expiry risk — return for credit note 3-month flag + sell-through

Auto-flags batches within 3 months of expiry. Prioritises by whether current sell-through will clear the batch in time; builds the return/credit-note action list per supplier.

Management roll-up value at risk

Stock value at risk, reorder volume and expiry exposure across the business.
Prototype by Jithin J · built to demonstrate the approach on sample data. In production this reads live from the ERP/SQL via API. Logic shown: reorder = demand over lead time + safety stock; expiry = flagged 3 months out, prioritised by sell-through; slow-moving = days-of-cover + trend.
